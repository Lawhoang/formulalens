Select a cell, then choose Precedents or Dependents. Select several cells to trace them together.

Colours are temporary. Clear the map to restore the original fills. Sheets over 10,000 cells are not mapped.

Select a calculation area (up to 2,000 cells), then analyse how data flows into and out of it.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Q dependents, Ctrl+Shift+Q precedents (press again on the same cell to clear), Alt+R refresh, Esc clear and hide; arrows, Space, Enter, Backspace navigate the tree (inside the panel). Highlighting changes cell fills, so Excel's undo history is cleared when you use it.
